# Flight Video Evidence

## FLT-001 — August 8, 2026

Full flight video documenting the first flight of the LOC Graduator using an Estes F15-4 motor.

[▶ Watch FLT-001 Flight Video on YouTube](https://youtu.be/gZYhUH88pAA)

## Video-Derived Flight Observations

- Successful liftoff and near-vertical ascent
- Estimated liftoff-to-apogee time: **~7.82 s**
- OpenRocket predicted time to apogee: **7.77 s**
- Prediction difference: **~0.05 s (~0.6%)**
- Recovery-system deployment observed immediately before apogee
- Successful parachute recovery
- Landing approximately **20 ft from the launch pad**
- Measurement method: post-flight video timestamp analysis

The video-derived timing is used as supporting flight evidence rather than onboard telemetry. No altimeter or flight computer was carried on this flight.
