Begin learning OpenRocket and create an initial rocket simulation before the physical kit arrives.

 02 — Kit Arrival, Inventory, and Pre-Build Measurements

## June 26–27, 2026

The LOC Graduator arrived in good condition. The first physical task was deliberately documentation-focused rather than immediately gluing parts together.

Completed work:

- Photographed the unopened kit and individual components.
- Checked the parts against the instructions.
- Performed an initial dry fit.
- Inspected tubes, fins, motor mount, coupler, recovery system, and hardware for visible damage.
- Recorded principal dimensions before sanding or adhesive could alter them.

The pre-build measurement set is retained in:

[`data/measurements/kit-baseline.csv'](data/measurements/kit-baseline.csv)

Representative measured geometry included:

- 508 mm slotted booster tube
- 254 mm payload tube
- 66.8 mm airframe OD
- 230 mm exposed nose-cone length
- 156 mm fin root chord
- 58 mm fin tip chord
- 105 mm fin span
- 152 mm motor-mount tube
- approximately 1016 mm overall dry-fit length

### Why this mattered

This created a clean reference point for later comparison with the completed rocket. It also separated geometry measurements from the much less predictable final mass, which would increase due to adhesive, epoxy, finish, hardware, and paint.
