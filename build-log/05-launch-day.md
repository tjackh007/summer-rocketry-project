# 05 — Launch Day

## August 8, 2026 — Hearne launch site

The first flight of INSTINCT used an **Estes F15-4** on a 1010 rail at the Hearne launch site.

### Conditions used for post-flight reconstruction

- Approximate launch time: 11:37 AM
- Clear sky
- Temperature: approximately 33.8 °C
- Average wind: approximately 2.77 m/s
- Wind direction: approximately 175°
- Elevation: approximately 87 m MSL
- Physical rail: approximately 10 ft
- Effective guided rail length used in OpenRocket: approximately 2.75 m

### Field adjustment

A minor fit/retention issue was discovered at the field and corrected with a small masking-tape adjustment before launch. The flight then proceeded normally. This became a useful lesson in completing a full hardware-fit check before leaving for the range.

### Flight observation

- Clean ignition and departure
- Nearly vertical ascent
- Parachute deployment
- Successful descent and recovery
- Landing roughly 20 ft from the launch pad
- No scratches or structural damage; only light dirt

The flight was a practical success and provided an important qualitative validation of the stability and recovery model.

### Limitation

No onboard altimeter was flown, so actual apogee, maximum velocity, and maximum acceleration were not measured. The post-flight comparison is therefore deliberately qualitative for those parameters.
