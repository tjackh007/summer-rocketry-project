# 06 — Post-Flight Simulation and Project Closeout

## August 10, 2026

After the successful flight, the project shifted from launch preparation to engineering closeout.

### Launch-day reconstruction

The final model was rerun using the reconstructed Hearne conditions and realistic rail geometry.

Launch-day prediction:

- Rail-exit velocity: 12.1 m/s
- Apogee: 203 m
- Deployment velocity: 13.0 m/s
- Optimum delay: 4.52 s
- Maximum velocity: 53.3 m/s
- Maximum acceleration: 35.3 m/s²
- Time to apogee: 7.77 s
- Total flight time: 37.4 s
- Ground-hit velocity: 7.40 m/s

The raw time history showed motor burnout at about 3.45 s, ejection at about 7.45 s, and apogee at about 7.77 s. That places modeled recovery deployment only about 0.32 s before apogee.

### Wind sensitivity

Four additional cases were run to understand the direction of performance change rather than to produce a single deterministic forecast.

The trend was consistent: stronger wind reduced apogee and increased deployment and landing velocity.

### Closeout decision

The project was considered complete after:

- physical build completion;
- measured mass and CG calibration;
- hardware-representative OpenRocket modeling;
- wind sensitivity and launch-day simulation;
- successful real flight;
- recovery without damage; and
- final GitHub documentation.
